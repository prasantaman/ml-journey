
### 🔍 Difficult / Thought-Provoking Questions (for deep thinking or advanced interviews):

1. **If a machine learning model performs well on training data but fails on new data, is it truly "learning"? Why or why not?**
2. **Can a model be 100% accurate on real-world data? If not, why?**
3. **What are the ethical challenges in training models on biased datasets?**
4. **How do you decide when enough data is "enough"?**
5. **Can unsupervised learning ever be more powerful than supervised learning? Give real-world cases.**
6. **What would happen if we use reinforcement learning in scenarios with no clear reward function?**
7. **Can a machine learn something a human cannot understand? Should we trust it in that case?**

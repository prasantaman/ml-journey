### ✅ **Definition for MNC Interviews:**

> **Machine Learning (ML)** is a subset of Artificial Intelligence that focuses on building systems that can **learn from data**, **identify patterns**, and **make decisions** with minimal human intervention. Instead of being explicitly programmed for every task, ML models **improve their performance over time** as they are exposed to more data.

---
> It involves algorithms that automatically improve through experience. Common types of machine learning include **supervised learning**, **unsupervised learning**, and **reinforcement learning**. Applications include **spam detection**, **fraud detection**, **recommendation systems**, and **predictive analytics**.
